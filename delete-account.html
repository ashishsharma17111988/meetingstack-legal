Delete your MeetingStack account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open MeetingStack and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is permanently deleted.

By email

If you can't access the app, email support@uvassolutions.com from the address registered to your account with the subject "Delete my MeetingStack account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account record (email, display name, photo reference, password hash).
  • Calendar data and sign-in tokens are stored only on your device; uninstalling the app removes them. We hold no copy.
  • Usage analytics events are tied to a random account ID, not your email. Once your account is deleted, that ID can no longer be linked to you; the events are not individually deletable.
