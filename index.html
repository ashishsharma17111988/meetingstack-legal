MeetingStack

Every meeting, one agenda. See your Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 / Outlook / Teams meetings together in one clean timeline.

Coming soon to Google Play.

What it does

  • One agenda for every calendar. Connect several Google and Microsoft accounts, each with its own colour.
  • No duplicates. A meeting invited through more than one account appears once.
  • Join in one tap. Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and Zoom links are detected automatically.
  • Respond and create. Accept, decline or tentatively accept invitations and create meetings without leaving the app.
  • Reminders. Meeting reminders are scheduled on your device.

Your calendar data and Google

MeetingStack asks for access to your Google Calendar events (calendar.events) and, for Microsoft accounts, Calendars.ReadWrite, only to show your meetings, let you respond to invitations and create events you ask for. Calendar data goes directly from Google or Microsoft to your phone. We do not store it on our servers, sell it, or use it for advertising.

MeetingStack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect any calendar in the app, and delete your MeetingStack account at any time.
